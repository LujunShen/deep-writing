# Style Audit Questionnaire

Complete the fields that are relevant. Use examples from your own writing wherever possible.

## Sentence And Punctuation

- Do you prefer short, medium, or long sentences?
- Do you deliberately alternate sentence lengths?
- How often do you use commas, semicolons, colons, dashes, and parentheses?
- Do you avoid a punctuation mark?

## Voice And Stance

- Do you use "I", "we", both, or neither? In which sections?
- Do you prefer active or passive voice? Does this change by section?
- Which tenses do you use in Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion?
- Do you hedge strongly, moderately, or rarely?
- Do you make evaluative judgments directly or cautiously?

## Argumentation

- Do you usually state the claim first or build evidence first?
- How do you open a paragraph?
- How do you close a paragraph?
- Which transitions do you use repeatedly?
- How do you express contrast, consequence, and emphasis?

## Vocabulary

- Which words or phrases are signature elements of your writing?
- Which academic phrases feel empty to you and should be avoided?
- Are there AI phrases you dislike, such as "notably", "it is important to note", or "furthermore"?

## Structure

- What do you consider a strong section order for a review or manuscript?
- Where do you place comparison, critique, and synthesis?
- How long are your typical paragraphs and sections?

## Hard Vetoes

- Which changes should Codex never make?
- Which changes require explicit approval before application?
