# Material Intake

Use this file to collect evidence before calibrating `style-profile.md`. Do not assume a default academic voice from one edited sample.

## Tier A: Required

1. **Original pre-AI drafts**: Three to five documents or substantial excerpts the author wrote without AI sentence rewriting. Full reviews are ideal; one Introduction, one Results/Analysis section, one Discussion, and one Methods section provide good coverage.
2. **Paired revisions**: At least two AI-revised versions matched to their originals. Ask the author to mark:
   - structural changes worth keeping;
   - structural changes that damaged the argument;
   - sentence edits that preserved the author's voice;
   - sentence edits that flattened it.
3. **Style anchors**: Fifteen to thirty verbatim original sentences or short passages the author considers representative. These are evidence of preferred rhythm, hedging, and critical stance.
4. **Anti-anchors**: Ten to twenty AI-produced or generic academic sentences the author rejects, each with a one-line reason.

## Tier B: Strongly Recommended

5. **Filled questionnaire**: `assets/style-audit-questionnaire.md`.
6. **Task context**: Document type, target journal, discipline, audience, and any journal-specific conventions.
7. **Hard vetoes**: Edits never to make, such as "do not change passive to active in Methods" or "do not remove 'we' in Discussion".

## Tier C: Useful When Available

8. **Structural exemplars**: One or two published papers the author admires for organization, labeled with what to imitate and what not to copy.
9. **Earlier feedback**: Notes from prior reviews, supervisor comments, or previous AI-editing sessions.
10. **Terminology list**: Approved spellings, acronyms, discipline-specific terms, and citation phrasing.

## Suggested Project Layout

```text
deep-writing-material/
  01_original/
  02_ai_edited/
  03_style_anchors.md
  04_anti_anchors.md
  05_questionnaire.md
  06_target_notes.md
  07_previous_feedback.md
```

Only the files that exist need to be loaded. Prefer source documents in their original format; if a file is large, extract relevant sections and record the source path in `style-profile.md`.
